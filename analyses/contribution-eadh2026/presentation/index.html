Introduction

Introduction

DH communities can be studied through presentation activity

Analyses of DH conference abstracts have become an established way of observing the field.

  • actors and institutions
  • thematic orientations
  • structural trends
  • citation and bibliometric networks
Problem

Each of these approaches captures only one segment of DH as an interdisciplinary research field.

Introduction

Lecture series add another perspective

Conference data

Public, contribution-based, often collaborative.

Lecture series data

Invitational, often local, connected to institutional contexts.

Our contribution combines DHd conference data with data on DH lecture series in Germany, Austria, and Switzerland.

Multiple authorship is common at conferences, while lecture-series events are usually delivered by a single speaker.

Analysis

Most people appear only once

Lecture series

12% of speakers deliver two lectures. The observed maximum is ten lectures by one person.

DHd conferences

18% of contributors are involved in two contributions. The observed maximum is 27 contributions by one person.

Multiple participation is somewhat more pronounced in the conference context, but the overall distribution patterns are broadly comparable.

Analysis

Analysis

Lecture series reveal parts of DH beyond conferences

The findings reflect the different social forms of the two formats.

  • conference contributions are self-submitted
  • lecture-series talks are usually invited
  • doctoral researchers are especially visible at conferences
  • lecture series often activate local actors
Interpretation

Lecture series function as points of connection between DH and other humanities communities at local institutions.

Analysis

A linkage score describes relative emphasis

linkage score = conference proportion / lecture-series proportion

Values above 1

stronger relative focus on conference contributions

Values approaching 0

stronger relative emphasis on lecture-series presentations

For the 257 people represented in both datasets, the median linkage score is 2.4.

04

Outlook

Outlook

DH appears as both linked and autonomous

By consolidating conference and lecture-series data, we can observe actors who are active in both settings, while also seeing separate groups that currently appear only in one context.

overlap
bridges
independent areas
Outlook

Further work requires richer and cleaner metadata

  • diachronic analysis of changing participation patterns
  • integration of institutional affiliations and locations
  • analysis of collaborative constellations
  • gender-related analysis of speakers and contributors
  • further authority-data enrichment
  • continued documentation of lecture series

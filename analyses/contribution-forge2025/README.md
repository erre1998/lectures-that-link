# Aus dem Off

This directory contains the research data and materials associated with the poster contribution "Aus dem Off: Ringvorlesungen und ihr Nutzen als Forschungsdatenquelle in den Digital Humanities", presented at the FORGE 2025 conference in Rostock, Germany.

The contribution explores the potential of lecture series and related event formats as sources of research data in the DH. Building on the [accompanying article published in *magazén*](https://doi.org/10.30687/mag/2724-3923/2025/02/001), it introduces the TEI-based XML data model and workflow developed as part of the research project "Lectures that Link".

In addition, the poster presents two exploratory visualizations examining the geographical distribution and temporal development of 46 DH-related lecture series identified in Germany, Austria, and Switzerland between 2010 and 2025. The underlying data were collected during the project's initial survey of relevant lecture series but had not yet been fully encoded and integrated into the main TEI dataset.

The conference abstract is available at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17178246, and the poster is available at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23618457.
